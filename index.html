About Us

Established in 2001, Marian Engineering College is one of the premier engineering institutes in the region, known for its academic excellence and world-class infrastructure.

The college offers a variety of undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering, with a focus on holistic development and industry-relevant skills.

Courses Offered

  • Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Science Engineering
  • Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Electronics and Communication Engineering
  • Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Civil Engineering
  • Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Mechanical Engineering
  • Master of Technology (M.Tech) in Computer Science
  • Master of Technology (M.Tech) in Structural Engineering
  • Master of Technology (M.Tech) in Power Systems

Facilities

Our college is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities to provide students with a conducive learning environment:

  • Modern laboratories with the latest tools and equipment
  • Fully equipped library with online resources
  • Sports complex and recreational facilities
  • Hostels with comfortable living conditions
  • Wi-Fi enabled campus with high-speed internet access
  • Transport facilities for students and staff

Admissions

Admissions to Marian Engineering College are based on the entrance exams and the merit list. We offer various scholarship programs for deserving students. Visit our admissions page for more details.
