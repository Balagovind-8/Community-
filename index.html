About Us

Established in 2001, Marian Engineering College is a leading institution for engineering studies in Kerala's self-financing sector. Affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU) and approved by AICTE, the college is situated on a sprawling 40-acre campus of Marian Educity near prominent institutions like the Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre and Technopark. Managed by the Trivandrum Social Service Society under the Trivandrum Latin Catholic Archdiocese, the college aims to cultivate professionals characterized by competence, confidence, commitment, and compassion.
